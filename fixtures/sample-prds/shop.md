# Sample Shop — Cart and shipping

The shop is a single page: a product list, a cart and a checkout form.

## Cart

Cart 1.1: Adding a product that is already in the cart increases that line's quantity by one instead of adding a second line.

Cart 1.2: Discount codes are case-insensitive: "SAVE10", "save10" and "Save10" all apply the same 10% discount.

Cart 1.3: When the last item is removed, the cart shows the message "Your cart is empty".

## Shipping

Shipping 2.1: Shipping costs $5.00 per order. Orders whose subtotal before discounts is $50.00 or more ship free. An empty cart shows $0.00 shipping.
